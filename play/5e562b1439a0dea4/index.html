How to play

One movie a day. Read the color timeline and use the year and genre to guess the title. Unlimited guesses. Aim for as few guesses as possible. An extra story clue is available. Revealing the answer ends today’s puzzle.

Each stripe is the average color of one frame sampled every 10 seconds, read from opening to credits. Your progress is kept on this device when browser storage is available.
